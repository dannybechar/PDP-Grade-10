# Teacher Materials

## Presentation

[`B1.1.1_Canvas_Coordinates.pptx`](B1.1.1_Canvas_Coordinates.pptx) is the classroom presentation for this lesson.

- Slides 1–13 form the planned 45-minute lesson.
- Slides 14–15 are recovery and troubleshooting appendices.
- Every slide includes speaker notes with timing, teacher moves, expected answers, and relevant sources.
- The complete program appendix should remain hidden until students have attempted the construction task.

Use the four projects in `../examples` at the checkpoints identified in the presentation.
